# Product design baseline

**Syntax - make your machine work in your language.** Search is the front door.
Syntax also exposes capabilities, performs work, remembers user-owned context
and makes every action visible and reusable.

The large command surface accepts natural language, search terms and slash
commands. It must never collapse into a tiny launcher box.

Modules: Search, Skills, Bring Your Own, Apps, MCP, Workers, Recipes, Scheduler,
History, Styles, Memory, Usage and Settings.

Every result exposes source, owner, truth state, evidence, relationship strength,
ranking explanation, last verification and next action. Users can create terms,
skills, recipes, styles, connections, workers and schedules. Paid editions
increase capacity, governance and shared capability-not one extra keyword.

Keyboard completeness, screen-reader labels, high-contrast states, reduced
motion, large targets and plain-language recovery are release gates.
